Privacy

Version 2026-08-04

This site is a personal research and advisory site. It collects personal data in exactly two places: the advisory enquiry form on /advisory/, and the newsletter subscription box on /writing/. Everything below describes those two flows.

Who is responsible#

The data controller is Sinclair (Po-Sung) Huang, operating this site in a personal capacity.

For any access, correction, or deletion request — or any question about this notice — write to research@sinclairhuang.org. Please say which of the two flows your request concerns so it can be located quickly.

Advisory enquiries#

What is collected. Your name, email address, a description of the question or situation you are writing about, and — if you choose to provide them — your company and expected timeline. The form also records which page the enquiry was sent from and the version of this notice in force at the time.

What it is used for. Replying to you, and assessing whether an engagement is appropriate. Nothing else.

An enquiry does not subscribe you to anything. Sending the form never adds you to the newsletter. There is no hidden or pre-ticked subscription field. If you also want the newsletter, subscribe separately on /writing/.

How long it is kept. Enquiry data is retained for at most 12 months after the last substantive contact, then deleted. The form provider’s dashboard (see below) holds submissions for 30 days on the current plan; copies forwarded to email are cleaned under the same 12-month policy. If an enquiry leads to a formal engagement, the records necessary for that engagement are kept for as long as contractual, accounting, and legal obligations require.

Please do not send in this form: trade secrets or confidential material, national identification numbers, medical or financial records, or personal data about other people. If your question depends on such material, say so in general terms and we can agree a suitable channel first.

Newsletter subscription#

Subscription is handled entirely by Substack, which is the single system of record for the mailing list. The subscription box on this site is Substack’s own embedded form, so your email address goes directly to Substack and is stored there under Substack’s privacy policy and terms.

Email confirmation is required. Subscriptions use double opt-in: Substack sends a confirmation email, and you are not added to the list until you confirm. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any issue.

What is not done with your data#

Your data is not sold, and submitted enquiry or subscription data is not used for advertising or cross-site profiling.

This is not a claim that no technical data is ever collected anywhere on this site. The embedded Substack subscription box and the anti-spam protection on the enquiry form are third-party components, and they may process technical information such as IP address, browser characteristics, and cookies under their own policies as part of delivering and protecting those functions.

Changes#

This notice is versioned by date. Enquiries record the version in force when they were sent, so it is always clear which terms applied. The current version is shown at the top of this page.
