Research Brief: The Illusion of Winning Streaks

A bilingual research brief of SSRN 7106058: why long no-loss streaks in retail Fixed Coupon Notes reflect market regime and tail deferral, not skill.
August 2, 2026

A bilingual research brief for The Illusion of Winning Streaks: Regime Dependence, Tail Deferral, and Survivorship Bias in Retail Fixed Coupon Notes — SSRN Working Paper No. 7106058, Huang, Po-Sung (Sinclair), 2026.

What the paper studies. A retail Fixed Coupon Note (FCN) is, in economic substance, a short position in a worst-of down-and-in put sold by the investor. Using Monte-Carlo simulation and a 2000–2026 historical backtest, the paper asks whether the long, uninterrupted profit streaks investors observe reflect any risk-adjusted superiority of the instrument — or the market regime they happened to sit in.

Three findings. First, streak illusion: in a strong bull regime the probability of two years with no loss is 78%, and the expected streak exceeds 32 quarterly periods. Second, tail-dominated expectation: a four-source decomposition shows positive returns come almost entirely from equity and variance risk premia while the issuer margin is a fixed negative drag, so net expected return is positive only in a strong bull. Third, fat left tail under regime switching: even conditioning on a bull start, the 5th-percentile five-year return is −71%.

The validation. On nine real semiconductor stocks (2000–2026, ~108,780 simulated contracts), realized assignment clusters in the crash years 2000–02, 2008, and 2022 (up to 54%); the two-year no-loss rate is still 66%; and a calendar-year block-bootstrap gives a 95% confidence interval for the five-year median return of [−1.8%, 41.0%] — straddling zero. A survivorship-bias stress test collapses the share of positive five-year outcomes from 64% to 27%.

The policy angle. Rather than wealth-based access gates, the paper proposes regime stress-disclosure and tail-tolerance suitability testing.

中文｜核心觀點#

散戶端的固定配息票據（FCN），在經濟實質上是投資人賣出的 worst-of 下敲入賣權的空頭部位。本文以蒙地卡羅模擬與 2000–2026 年歷史回測提出三個發現：其一，「連勝幻覺」——在強勢多頭下，連續兩年不虧損的機率高達 78%，預期連勝超過 32 個季度；其二，「尾部主導的期望值」——正報酬幾乎全部來自股權與波動風險溢酬，發行商利差是固定的負向拖累，只有在強多頭中淨期望報酬才為正；其三，「制度切換下的肥左尾」——即使從多頭起步，五年期報酬的第 5 百分位仍為 −71%。

在九檔半導體實股（約 108,780 份合約）的驗證中，履約集中在 2000–02、2008 與 2022 的崩盤年份（最高 54%）；生存者偏誤壓力測試把五年期正報酬的比例從 64% 壓到 27%。政策面，本文主張以「制度壓力揭露」與「尾部承受度適足性測試」取代以財力門檻限制參與。
